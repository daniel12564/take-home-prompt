- **02, seed and selectors:** Account and Location models, the seed converter and `seed_activity` command (idempotent, `--flush`, original ids preserved), the accounts and locations endpoints, and the header selectors. Account, week and Location are kept in the URL.
- **03, Reporting Week:** the Reference Date setting, timezone-aware week bounds, the week picker, and `week_start` / `location` validation on the Activity Summary endpoint.
- **04, counts:** weekly counts for the three Metrics, from one grouped query.
- **05, Baseline and Status:** median of the trailing eight complete weeks and the combined Tolerance (ADR-0001). The Activity Summary shows typical, above, below or insufficient data. All rule constants are in `DASHBOARD_RULES`.
- **06, Attention List:** per-Location Findings, a No-activity Finding, Severity, ordering and plain-language explanations (ADR-0002).
- **07, Account-wide Findings:** rollup when most Locations move together, plus a one-day callout.
- **08, acceptance and README:** every PLAN §3.13 row was walked against the real seed, and the README covers clone-to-demo steps, rules and known limitations.

Tickets 02–08 are marked resolved in `.scratch/`. Each ticket's `## Answer` section records its deviations.

## Review follow-ups

A standards and spec review of the branch led to these fixes:
- The UI shows per-field validation messages, for example an unknown `location`.
- The one-day callout is tested per Metric, not pooled across Metrics.
- Above-typical Findings use amber styling, even when their Severity is critical.
- Shared frontend formatting moved to `lib/format.ts`, and a stale comment was removed.

## Deviations from the plan

- **North fixture:** the plan has 12 calls against a baseline of 5. That makes the Finding `critical` under the §3.6 severity rule, but the ticket requires `warning`. The fixture now uses 11 calls, and the plan and spec say so.
- **Account-wide baseline:** the Finding now quotes the Account's total baseline, matching the Summary card (40.5).
- **Insufficient data:** the Summary says "no activity recorded" for an Account with no events, instead of the short-history text.
- **Extra warnings on account 6:** on 2026-06-29 and 2026-04-13, zero-baseline Locations produce warnings beyond the §3.13 checklist. The plan and README now list the full result.
- **New payload fields:** `insufficient_reason` and `affected_by_metric`.

## Open questions

- An invalid `week` in the URL silently falls back to the default week. Ticket 03 asked for that, but spec story 39 says a mistyped link should not silently show something else.
- Zero-baseline Findings sort ahead of larger real deviations in their tier.
- A surge can show amber with a "Critical" label.
- The merged Account-wide explanation only gives numbers for the first Metric.
- User-facing text uses "this week" and "Typical", which are on the glossary's avoid list.
